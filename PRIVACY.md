# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Spread is a document editor that runs in your browser or as a desktop app. This policy explains what data it touches, which is very little.

## Your documents

Your files stay on your computer. Spread opens and saves .docx files using your browser's file picker or the desktop app's file dialogs. Document contents are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored anywhere other than where you save them. There is no server that could receive them. The web version is a static site, and the app works offline after the first load.

## Accounts

There are none. Spread has no sign up, no login, and no way to identify you.

## What Spread stores locally

Spread keeps a small amount of data in your browser's local storage on your own machine:

- Settings, such as theme, highlight color, and reader speeds.
- A list of recently opened files, so the Recent section on the home screen works. In browsers that support it, this includes file handles that let Spread ask your browser for permission to reopen a file. Your browser shows a permission prompt before Spread can read or write any file on that list.

You can clear the recents list from the home screen, and clearing your browser's site data removes everything.

## Analytics and tracking

Spread contains no analytics, no telemetry, no error reporting, no ads, and no tracking of any kind. We do not know who uses it or how.

## Network requests the web version makes

Loading the site at wowneutral.github.io fetches the app's files from GitHub Pages, and the page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those services receive the standard information any web request carries, such as your IP address, and handle it under their own policies: [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). After the first visit a service worker caches the app so it can run offline. The desktop app makes no network requests at all.

## Children

Spread is usable by students of any age precisely because it collects nothing.
